# ISO 27001 Client Discovery Questionnaire

> **Purpose:** This questionnaire is used during the initial discovery session of an ISO 27001 audit engagement. Answers are used to assess the current state of the client's information security management system (ISMS) and to populate applicable security policies.
>
> **Instructions:** Complete as much as possible before the opening meeting. Items left blank will be discussed during the session. Attach supporting documents (org charts, process diagrams, existing policies) where available.

---

## What this questionnaire covers

The full document runs 79 numbered questions across 13 sections, each mapped to a specific ISO 27001 clause or Annex A control area. It starts with client intake and engagement logistics, then walks through every domain an ISO 27001 audit needs to assess.

| Section | Focus | Maps to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client Details, Point Persons, Engagement Scope and Logistics, Kickoff Discussion | Engagement setup |
| 2 | Organizational Context and Leadership | ISO 27001 Clauses 4, 5, 6 |
| 3 | Human Resources and People Security | Annex A.6 |
| 4 | Physical and Environmental Security | Annex A.7 |
| 5 | Asset Management | Annex A.5.9-5.12 |
| 6 | Supplier and Third-Party Management | Annex A.5.19-5.22 |
| 7 | Compliance, Legal, and Policy Management | Clause 4.2, Annex A.5.31-5.34 |
| 8 | Incident Management and Business Continuity | Annex A.5.24-5.30 |
| 9 | Access Control and Identity Management | Annex A.5.15-5.18, A.8.2-8.5 |
