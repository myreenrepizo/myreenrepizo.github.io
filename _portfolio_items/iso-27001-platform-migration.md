# ISO 27001 Compliance Migration: Manual Documentation to Automated Platform

**Client type:** SaaS company pursuing ISO 27001 certification
**Engagement:** Ongoing
**Platform:** Vanta
**Details:** Client specifics withheld per engagement confidentiality.

---

## The situation

The client's ISO 27001 compliance program was managed manually: policies, control evidence, and
audit documentation scattered across a shared drive, with no continuous tracking and no automated
evidence collection. Every audit cycle meant re-gathering evidence from scratch.

## The work

Leading the migration of the full ISO 27001 program from manual document storage into Vanta, a
dedicated GRC automation platform:

- Importing relevant policies and documents into Vanta and enabling the platform's regular approval workflow
- Mapping existing policies and evidence to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls inside the platform
- Building integrations that pull evidence directly from source systems instead of manual uploads
- Transforming the existing risk register spreadsheet to importing it to the platform's risk register
- Assigning role-based onboarding and offboarding tasks, including approval of specific policies and relevant training
- Redesigning documentation workflows so control evidence stays current without a manual quarterly push
- Automating recurring compliance tasks (access reviews, policy attestations, and control
  monitoring) that were previously manual, calendar-driven processes

## Why it matters

Moving from static documents to Vanta turns ISO 27001 from a once-a-year audit scramble into a
program that stays current between audits: less manual upkeep, fewer gaps discovered at audit
time, and a continuous evidence trail instead of a pre-audit fire drill.
