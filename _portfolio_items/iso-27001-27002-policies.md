> **Standard references:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · ISO/IEC 27002:2022 · ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd.1:2024
> **Policy types:** Overarching (top management approval) · Topic-Specific (appropriate management level)

---

## Overarching Policy

| Policy | Type | Referenced In | Contains / Controls to Address |
|--------|------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| **Information Security Policy** | Overarching | ISO 27001 Cl. 5.2; ISO 27002 5.1 | High-level statement of intent covering: organizational context and scope (ISO 27001 Cl. 4), leadership commitment (Cl. 5), planning and risk treatment (Cl. 6), support and resources (Cl. 7), operational controls (Cl. 8), performance evaluation (Cl. 9), continual improvement (Cl. 10). Sets objectives, principles, roles and responsibilities, and exceptions framework for all topic-specific policies. |

## Note on Climate Change (ISO 27001:2022/Amd.1:2024)

Amendment 1 does not mandate a standalone climate change policy. However, if the organization determines that climate change **is a relevant issue** (Clause 4.1), the following existing policies must be updated to incorporate climate-related risks:

| Policy to Update | Climate Considerations to Add |
|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| **Physical and Environmental Security Policy** | Risks from extreme weather events, flooding, heat, or other climate-driven threats to facilities and equipment (aligns with 7.5 -- Protecting against physical and environmental threats) |
| **Information Security Incident Management Policy** | Climate-driven disruption scenarios (e.g., natural disasters) as incident triggers; continuity procedures for climate events |
| **Backup Policy** | Off-site/cloud backup requirements driven by climate-risk to primary site |
