## Note on Climate Change (ISO 27001:2022/Amd.1:2024)

Amendment 1 does not mandate a standalone climate change policy. However, if the organization determines that climate change **is a relevant issue** (Clause 4.1), several existing policies (Physical and Environmental Security, Information Security Incident Management, Backup) need to be updated to incorporate climate-related risks. The full inventory includes the specific clause-by-clause guidance for each.
